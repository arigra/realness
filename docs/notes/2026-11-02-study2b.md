# Study 2 on the tuned simulators, Study 2b, and the label budget (pre-registered 2026-11-02, before any run)

## What changed since the Study 2 pre-registration (2026-10-31)
- Every family runs on each dataset's **best tuned simulator** (docs/notes/2026-11-02-simulators.md),
  not the spec-only one: tolerances measured around a poor simulator say little about a good one.
  "clean" below = that simulator.
- RADDet uses a **scene-disjoint split** (scripts/split_raddet.py): the official split interleaves
  train and test frames of the same scenes, and the tuned simulator draws its scenes from real
  training labels.
- **Roles of the real data.** The 50 real training sequences tune the simulators and nothing
  else; 32 val sequences early-stop training; the whole val split is what any choice is made on;
  test is reported, never chosen on.
- Families and levels of Study 2 are unchanged. Defects that were re-rendered on the spec-only
  simulator are now mechanisms of the tuned one (src/damage.py: TrackDamage, RadialDamage); the
  strength families move the tuned offsets further (noise +d dB = targets and static scene both
  -d dB, as maps are normalised).

## Study 2b: the four gaps found on 2026-11-02
| family | levels (mild -> extreme) | how |
|---|---|---|
| support | 25 50 75 100% | share of sequences whose objects come from the simulator's own scenario instead of a real frame's labels |
| annotation | +6 +12 +18 dB | objects whose strongest return is below (fitted threshold, or 0 dB if none) + level are rendered but not labelled |
| tail | 2 4 8 16 cells | target power smeared away from the radar in range (one-sided exponential, this decay length) |
| confusers | x0 x0.5 x2 x4 | number of discrete static structures (poles, parked cars; RADIal: structure coverage and trees) |

Same settings as Study 2: synthetic only, 3 seeds, AP@2 primary, AP@4 and recall@2 reported.

## Readout (as Study 2)
Per family, mean AP (3 seeds) against strength; noise = median seed range over all versions of the
dataset; onset = the mildest level whose mean is more than noise below clean and stays so at every
stronger level, else none. Reported on test (as pre-registered) and on full val. Cross-dataset:
per family, whether onsets agree (same level +/-1 step) on all three datasets.

## Label budget
Real-only and tuned-simulator + real ("mix": half of each batch real), with 1, 5, 10, 25 and 50 of
the 50 real training sequences (evenly spaced), 3 seeds; 0 = synthetic only (Study 2's clean).
Readout: AP against budget for both, and the budget where mix stops beating real-only by more than noise.
